## 📦 Installing and running Compare Text

### Requirements

- Windows 11 or Windows 10 version 2004 (code name 20H1 / build number 19041) or newer.
- x64 or ARM64 processor

### Via Microsoft Store

Install from the [Microsoft Store's Compare Text page][microsoft-store-link].


## ℹ️ About

Protect your work against plagiarism, verify contract clauses against official templates, audit source code changes, or review academic drafts with total confidence.

With complete privacy: nothing leaves your computer.

🧠 Smart Analysis Powered by AI and Specialized Agents

Don't just see what changed—understand why it matters. Compare Text integrates Artificial Intelligence assistants that read and analyze differences based on your industry's context:

- Generic Agent: Clear summaries and direct explanations of changes.

- Legal Agent: Detects legal nuances, clause risks, and contractual changes.

- Editor Agent: Articles, books, blogs. Evaluates shifts in tone, clarity, precision, and editorial style.

- Technical Agent: Ideal for manuals, specifications, and documentation.

- Code Agent: Designed for developers and code diff reviews.

- Academic Agent: Identifies changes in citations, structure, and phrasing in research papers.

- Journalistic Agent: Analyzes news editing, sources, and editorial style.

- Financial Agent: Evaluates variations in economic reports and balance sheets.

🛡️ Total Privacy and Flexibility in AI

Text comparison is performed entirely inside your device. Complete privacy. Keep your data safe. Subsequent AI analysis is optional.

- Free Mode (100% Private): Uses Ollama to run AI models locally on your computer, without your data ever leaving your device. Includes the Generic and Legal agents.

- Plus Subscription (BYOK - Bring Your Own Key): Connect your direct API keys from OpenAI, Gemini, Anthropic, Groq, or Grok (xAI) and unlock the full catalog of agents.

- Premium Subscription (Managed AI): Full, immediate access with managed keys requiring no setup, plus support for your own API keys.

🗃️ Reports and History
Save your findings and AI-generated analysis reports in professional formats (HTML, plain text, and JSON). Access your analysis history at any time to retrieve previous comparisons.

Fully customizable, featuring a simple, accessible, and intuitive interface.
